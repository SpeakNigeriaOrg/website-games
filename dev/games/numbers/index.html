Yoruba number pictures

Each picture is drawn from how the Yoruba word is formed. Every number is drawn on the same ring, which holds four groups of five: a full ring is one score (20). 1 to 9 fill it group by group, 10 fills two groups, two rings linked like a chain are 30 (ọgbọ̀n, a word of its own), and a heap is 200 (igba). Glowing cowries are added (11 oókànlá, "one on ten"): in the next group of a ring with room, or on a new ring after a full one (23 = a full ring and a new ring of three). Empty places in a loop are taken away (15 ẹ̀ẹ́dógún, "five from twenty"). Loops side by side are twenties (40 ogójì, 20 × 2).
