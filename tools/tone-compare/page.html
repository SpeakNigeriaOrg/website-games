Tone synthesis comparison

Every column plays the same pitches and the same measured contours — only the timbre differs. Rendered from the game's own buildToneGraph, so what you hear is what the game plays. original is the frozen six-sine version kept as a reference point. Best judged on a phone speaker, not headphones.

How to read the numbers. Brightness is the spectral centroid — where the energy sits on average. Crest is peak divided by average level; it tracked the harshness in listening tests, but it is a proxy, not a perceptual measure. The ear decides; these only say which direction a change moves in.

What no variant changes: the pitches and the contours. Four of the six glides fall and the low-to-high span is 325–353 cents, between a minor and a major third. That is what was measured from the speakers' recordings, and it is why these sound plaintive whatever the timbre. Widening it would teach something untrue — the honest lever for more drama is TONE_SPREAD, which exaggerates the glides without moving the pitches apart.
