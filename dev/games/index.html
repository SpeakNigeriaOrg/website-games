Yorùbá Games Lab

Experimental games for young learners, built on Femi's recordings. Each game is a different core mechanic: hear and choose, catch the word, act it out, drum the syllables, or just explore with no wrong answer.

Ọ̀nà ẹ̀kọ́: the learning pathThe games strung into a course: scenes from a child's day (after the curriculum draft), a few new words at a time, earlier words coming back.
Local development build: not deployed. Audio is the v5 processor's output, exported locally; pictures marked provisional are the judge's picks, not yet reviewed.
